# General Manager Overview

As a General Manager, you have full access to the system. You can see everything, manage everyone, and make all the big decisions.

## What you can do

- **Dashboard Access** (https://workflow.remobile.eu/dashboard) - See charts and numbers about business performance. Track how many phones we're processing, repair success rates, and team productivity.
- **User Management** (https://workflow.remobile.eu/admin/users) - Add new employees, change their roles, and remove people who leave the company.
- **Full System Access** - You can use every part of the system: batch intake, repairs, quality control, inventory, and reporting.
- **Inventory Management** - Check spare parts stock, see what we need to order, and manage suppliers.


## Your typical responsibilities

- **Daily:** Check dashboard for yesterday's numbers, review any problems or delays
- **Weekly:** Look at team performance, check inventory levels, review quality metrics
- **Monthly:** Analyze trends, plan for busy periods, review supplier performance
- **As needed:** Add new employees, handle system problems, make policy decisions

## What makes your role different

You can:
- See some financial information
- Access all areas of the system
- Create and modify user accounts
- View detailed business reports on Dashboard


## Remember
With great power comes great responsibility! You can see information and make changes that affect everyone. Use your access wisely and keep confidential information secure.
