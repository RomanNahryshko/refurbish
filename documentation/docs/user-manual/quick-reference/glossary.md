# Glossary

Common terms you'll hear and see in the ReMobile system.

## Phone identification

**IMEI** - 15-digit number that uniquely identifies every phone in the world. Usually starts with 35 or 86.

**Internal ID** - 8-digit number our system creates for each phone (like 00001234). Printed on labels.

**Serial Number** - Phone manufacturer's unique number. Different from IMEI.

## Repair terms

**Housing Change** - Replacing the back cover or frame of a phone. L1 technicians only.

**Glass Change** - Replacing the front screen or glass. L2 technicians only.

**Battery Change** - Replacing the phone's battery. L3 technicians only.

**Software Update** - Updating the phone's operating system. Any technician can do this.

**Dr. Phone** - Software we use to scan phones and check their condition automatically.

## Quality control

**Initial QC** - First quality check when phones arrive. Operations managers do this.

**Final QC** - Quality check after repairs are done. QC controllers do this.

**Grade A** - Best condition phones, look almost new.

**Grade B** - Good condition phones, some minor wear.

**Grade C** - Acceptable condition phones, visible wear but everything works.

## User roles

**L1 Technician** - Can only do housing repairs and software updates.

**L2 Technician** - Can only do glass repairs and software updates.

**L3 Technician** - Can do battery, other repairs, and software updates.

**Operations Manager** - Handles batch intake and creates repair jobs.

**QC Controller** - Does final quality control and assigns grades.

**General Manager** - Can access everything and manage users.

## System terms

**Batch** - A group of phones that arrived together from a supplier.

**Repair Job** - A specific task to fix one problem on one phone.

**Queue** - List of jobs waiting to be done.

**Spare Parts** - Replacement parts we use for repairs (screens, batteries, etc.).

**Stock Level** - How many spare parts we have in inventory.
