# Who to Ask for Help

When you need help, here's who to contact for different problems.

## System problems

**Can't log in?**
→ Ask your General Manager to reset your password

**System is slow or not working?**
→ Tell your General Manager - they handle technical issues

**Lost your temporary password?**
→ Ask your General Manager for a new one

## Job-specific questions

**Operations Manager questions:**
- How to handle difficult Dr. Phone imports
- Which repairs to create for unusual problems
- Supplier or invoice questions
→ Ask your General Manager or experienced Operations Manager

**Technician questions:**
- How to do a specific repair
- Which parts to use for a phone model
- Tool or equipment problems
→ Ask an L3 technician or your supervisor

**QC Controller questions:**
- How to grade borderline phones
- What to do with phones that have multiple small problems
- Quality standards questions
→ Ask your General Manager or experienced QC Controller

## Training and learning

**New to your job?**
→ Ask to shadow an experienced person in your role for a few days

**Want to learn new skills?**
→ Talk to your General Manager about training opportunities

**Confused about a process?**
→ Ask anyone who's been doing that job longer than you

## Emergency situations

**Phone caught fire or is smoking?**
→ Unplug it immediately, move it away from other phones, tell everyone nearby

**Someone got hurt?**
→ Get first aid help immediately, then tell your General Manager

**System shows wrong information that affects money or customers?**
→ Tell your General Manager right away

## General rule

**When in doubt, ask!** It's better to ask a question than to make a mistake. Everyone was new once, and experienced people are usually happy to help.

**Don't guess** - especially with repairs, quality control, or anything involving customer phones.
