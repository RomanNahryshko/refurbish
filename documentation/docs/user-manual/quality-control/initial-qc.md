# Initial Quality Control

This page explains initial QC that happens during batch intake. Operations managers do this, not QC controllers.

## What initial QC is
When new phones arrive, operations managers check each one to decide what it needs:
- Does it need repairs?
- Is it good enough to get a grade right away?

## When this happens
- Right after Dr. Phone scanning
- During the batch intake process
- Before phones go to technicians

## What gets checked
- **Screen condition** - cracks, scratches, dead pixels
- **Housing condition** - dents, scratches, missing pieces  
- **Battery health** - does it hold charge?
- **Basic functions** - does it turn on, charge, make calls?
- **Physical damage** - water damage, bent frame, etc.

## Two outcomes

**Needs Repairs** - Phone goes to repair queue with specific jobs (housing, glass, battery, etc.)

**Ready for Grading** - Phone gets a grade (A, B, or C) and is ready to sell

## What happens next
- Phones needing repairs go to technicians
- Phones with grades go straight to inventory
- All phones get labels printed for tracking

## Note for QC controllers
You don't do initial QC - that's the operations manager's job. You only do final QC after repairs are finished. But it's good to understand the whole process!
