# Quality Control Overview

As a QC Controller, you're the final judge of phone quality. You decide if phones are ready to sell and what grade they get.

## What you do

**Final Quality Control** - Check phones after technicians finish repairs

**Grade Assignment** - Give each phone a grade: A (best), B (good), or C (acceptable)

**Trigger Repairs** - When you fail a device and select needed repairs, the system automatically creates repair jobs

## Your workflow

1. Check your queue for phones ready for Final QC
2. Test each phone using the QC checklist
3. **Pass?** → Assign grade (A, B, or C) → Goes to inventory
4. **Fail?** → Select needed repairs → System creates repair jobs automatically

## Remember
You're the last person to touch phones before they go to customers. Your decisions directly affect customer satisfaction and our reputation!
