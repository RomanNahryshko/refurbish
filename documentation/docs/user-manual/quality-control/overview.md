# Quality Control Overview

As a QC Controller, you're the final judge of phone quality. You decide if phones are ready to sell and what grade they get.

## What you do

**Final Quality Control** - Check phones after technicians finish repairs. This is your main job.

**Grade Assignment** - Give each phone a grade: A (best), B (good), or C (acceptable).

**Pass/Fail Decisions** - Decide if repairs were done properly or if phones need more work.

**Quality Standards** - Make sure every phone meets our standards before it goes to customers.

## Your typical day

1. **Check your queue** - see which phones finished repairs
2. **Test each phone** - run through your quality checklist
3. **Grade good phones** - A, B, or C based on condition
4. **Send back problems** - phones that need more repair work
5. **Keep records** - document what you found and decided

## What you can do
- Access the Quality Control page
- Test phones that finished repairs
- Assign final grades (A, B, C)
- Send phones back for more repairs
- View device history and repair details

## What you can't do
- Create repair jobs (that's for operations managers)
- Manage inventory or spare parts
- Do initial quality control on new batches

## Remember
You're the last person to touch phones before they go to customers. Your decisions directly affect customer satisfaction and our reputation!
