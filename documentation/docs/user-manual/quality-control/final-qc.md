# Final Quality Control

This page shows you how to check phones after technicians finish repairs.

## When you need this
Every time a phone shows "Final QC" status - meaning repairs are done and it needs your approval.

## Steps for final QC

1. Go to "Quality Control" in the main menu
2. You'll see a list of phones ready for final QC
3. Click on a phone to start checking it
4. Run through your quality checklist (see QC Checklist page)
5. Make your decision:
   - **Pass** - phone is ready, assign a grade
   - **Fail** - phone needs more work, send back to repairs

## What to check

**Physical condition:**
- Screen is perfect (no cracks, scratches, dead pixels)
- Housing looks good (no dents, scratches, gaps)
- All buttons work properly
- Charging port works
- Camera lenses are clean and uncracked

**Software functions:**
- Phone turns on and off properly
- Touch screen responds everywhere
- All apps work
- WiFi and cellular connect
- Camera takes clear photos
- Speaker and microphone work

**Battery performance:**
- Charges to 100%
- Holds charge properly
- No overheating while charging

## Making your decision

**If everything looks good:** Give it a grade (A, B, or C) and mark as "Pass"

**If something is wrong:** Mark as "Fail" and write notes about what needs fixing

## What happens next
- **Passed phones** get their final grade and go to inventory
- **Failed phones** go back to repair queue with your notes
- The system tracks your decisions for quality reports

## Pro tips
- **Be thorough** - customers will notice problems you miss
- **Write clear notes** - help technicians understand what to fix
- **Don't rush** - quality is more important than speed
- **When in doubt, fail it** - better safe than sorry
