# Final QC Process

Check phones after technicians complete repairs.

## Steps

1. Go to "Quality Control" in the main menu
2. Click on a phone ready for Final QC
3. Run through the QC Checklist (see QC Checklist page for full details)
4. Make your decision

## Your decision

**✅ Pass:**
- Assign grade (A, B, or C)
- Phone goes to inventory

**❌ Fail:**
- Select needed repairs (checkboxes)
- Add notes about problems
- **System automatically creates repair jobs** (just like Initial QC)
- Phone returns to repair queue

## Important
The process works exactly like Initial QC during batch intake - when you select repair checkboxes and save, jobs are created instantly. You're not "requesting" repairs, you're triggering them automatically.
