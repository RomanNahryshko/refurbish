# Completing Repairs

This page shows you how to finish a repair job and send the phone to quality control.

## When you need this
After you've fixed the phone and tested that everything works properly.

## Steps to complete a repair

1. **Test your work thoroughly:**
   - Turn the phone on and off
   - Test all functions related to your repair
   - Make sure nothing else broke during repair
   - Check that the phone looks good

2. **Go to Repair Jobs page**
3. **Find your job** in the "In Progress" section
4. **Click "Complete Repair"**
5. **Record parts used:**
   - Select each part you used from the dropdown
   - Enter how many of each part
   - Add notes if needed (like "screen was cracked worse than expected")
6. **Add completion notes:**
   - Describe what you did
   - Mention any problems you found
   - Note if anything else needs attention
7. **Click "Mark Complete"**

## What to include in your notes

**Good examples:**
- "Replaced cracked housing, tested all buttons - everything works"
- "New battery installed, phone charges properly, no overheating"
- "Screen replacement complete, touch works everywhere, no dead pixels"

**Bad examples:**
- "Fixed" (too short, no details)
- "Housing done" (doesn't say if you tested it)

## Recording parts used

**Why this matters:** The system tracks inventory and costs. If you don't record parts, we run out of stock without knowing.

**Be accurate:** Only record parts you actually used. If you damaged a part during repair, record it too.

**Ask if unsure:** If you're not sure what part name to use, ask your supervisor.

## What happens next
- The phone goes to "Final QC" status
- QC staff will test your work
- If they find problems, the phone comes back to you with notes
- If everything looks good, the phone gets graded and goes to inventory

## Common problems
- **Forgot to test something?** Better to catch it now than have QC send it back
- **Used wrong part?** Tell your supervisor - they can help fix the inventory
- **Something else broke?** Create a note so QC knows it wasn't your fault

## Pro tips
- **Test everything twice** - it's embarrassing when QC finds obvious problems
- **Take photos** - if you found extra damage, photos help explain your notes
- **Be honest** - if you couldn't fix something perfectly, say so in your notes
