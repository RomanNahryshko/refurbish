# Technician Overview

As a technician, you fix phones so they work like new again. You pick your own jobs and work at your own pace.

## Your technician level

**L1 Technician - Housing Specialist**
- You only do housing changes (back cover repairs)
- You can't do glass or battery work
- You can do software updates

**L2 Technician - Glass Specialist**  
- You only do glass changes (screen repairs)
- You can't do housing or battery work
- You can do software updates

**L3 Technician - Advanced Repairs**
- You do battery changes
- You handle "other" repairs (camera, speaker, charging port, etc.)
- You can do software updates
- You're the most skilled technician level

## Your typical day

1. **Check repair queue** - see what jobs are available for your level
2. **Pick a job** - choose something you want to work on
3. **Find the phone** - use the Internal ID on the printed label
4. **Do the repair** - replace the broken part with a new one
5. **Test your work** - make sure everything works properly
6. **Record parts used** - tell the system what parts you used
7. **Mark job complete** - send the phone to final QC
8. **Repeat** - pick your next job

## What you can do
- View repair jobs for your skill level
- Claim jobs you want to work on
- Mark jobs as complete when finished
- Record which spare parts you used
- View device details and repair history

## What you can't do
- Take jobs above your skill level
- Create new repair jobs
- Do final quality control
- Manage inventory or order parts

## Remember
- **Quality first** - take your time to do good work
- **Test everything** - make sure repairs actually work
- **Be honest** - if you can't fix something, say so
- **Keep learning** - ask questions and improve your skills!
