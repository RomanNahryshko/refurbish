# Reassigning Repairs

This page shows you how to move repair jobs between technicians.

## When you need this
- A technician is sick or busy
- Jobs are piling up for one person
- You need to balance the workload
- A technician started a job but can't finish it

## Steps to reassign a repair

1. Go to "Repair Jobs" in the main menu
2. Find the job you want to move
3. Click on the job to open it
4. Look for "Assigned To" section
5. Click "Change Assignment"
6. Choose the new technician from the dropdown
7. Add a note explaining why (optional but helpful)
8. Click "Save Changes"

## What you can reassign

**Pending jobs** - Easy to move, no work started yet

**In-progress jobs** - Be careful! Talk to both technicians first

**Completed jobs** - You can't move these

## Rules to remember

- **L1 technicians** can only do housing repairs
- **L2 technicians** can only do glass repairs  
- **L3 technicians** can do battery, software, and other repairs
- **Any technician** can do software updates

## What happens next
- The new technician sees the job in their queue
- The old technician loses access to the job
- The system tracks who did what for history

## Best practices
- **Talk to people first** - don't surprise technicians with new work
- **Check skill levels** - make sure the new person can do the job
- **Balance workload** - don't give everything to your fastest worker
- **Add notes** - explain why you moved the job so others understand
