# Navigation Overview

This page explains the main menu items you'll see at the top of your screen.

## When you need this
Every time you use the system - the menu is always there to help you move around.

## Main menu items

**Dashboard** - Only managers see this. Shows numbers and charts about how the business is doing.

**Batch Intake** - Where new phone batches get registered. Operations managers use this most.

**Devices** - Search for any phone by IMEI or Internal ID. Everyone can use this to find phones.

**Repair Jobs** - Technicians use this to pick repair work. Managers use it to assign jobs.

**Quality Control** - QC staff use this to check phones after repairs and give them grades.

**Inventory** - Managers check spare parts stock here. Shows what parts we have and need.

## What you'll see
Your menu only shows what you need for your job. Don't worry if you don't see everything - that's how it should be!

## Tips
- Click "Devices" when you need to find a specific phone
- Your most-used menu item will depend on your job
