# Finding Devices

https://workflow.remobile.eu/devices - this page shows you how to search for any phone in the system.

## When you need this
- Looking for a specific phone a customer asked about
- Checking the status of a phone you're working on
- Finding phones from a particular batch

## Steps to find a device

1. Click "Devices" in the main menu
2. Look for the search box at the top
3. Type one of these:
   - **IMEI** (15 digits, usually starts with 35 or 86)
   - **Internal ID** (8 digits like 00001234)
   - **Phone model** (like "iPhone 12" or "Samsung S21")
4. Press Enter or click the search button

## What the search finds
The system looks through:
- IMEI numbers
- Internal IDs  
- Phone models
- Serial numbers

## What happens next
You'll see a list of phones that match your search. Click on any phone to see:
- Its current status (received, in repair, etc.)
- What repairs it needs
- Its quality grade
- Complete history of what happened to it

## Pro tips
- **IMEI is fastest** - if you have it, use it
- **Internal ID works great** - it's on every label we print
- **Can't find it?** Try typing just part of the model name
- **Still stuck?** Check if you typed the numbers correctly
